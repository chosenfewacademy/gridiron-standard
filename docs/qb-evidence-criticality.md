# QB Evidence Criticality and Calendar Resolution

This policy extends the Architecture v1.0 implementation without changing its rating, scarcity, approval, positional baseline, or OVR rules.

Calendar resolution uses fresh official NFL evidence first, a valid cached official boundary second, and another stored official same-season boundary third. Retrieval age and boundary validity are separate. Immutable preseason/offseason boundaries may survive retrieval aging. Mutable regular-season game counts still require fresh evidence. Malformed, out-of-coverage, nonofficial, or materially conflicting sources fail closed. Stored provenance includes URL, retrieval time, season and boundary validity where supplied, authority class where supplied, extracted boundary dates, and a computed SHA-256 boundary fingerprint.

QB evidence uses four levels. `CORE_BLOCKING` covers current role and availability, passing quality, accuracy by depth, arm talent, pressure, decisions and mistakes, pocket/sack behavior, trajectory, and positional controls. `CORE_SUPPORTING` covers play action, throws on run, scrambling, designed rushing, ball security, postseason, and opponent context; a gap proceeds only through an approved bounded substitute. `SECONDARY_TRAIT` covers SPD, ACC, AGI, COD, STR, JMP, STA, and TGH; each final value remains required, but historical measurement, current play evidence, or conservative career-profile inference may provide a lower-confidence path. `CONTEXTUAL` evidence aids interpretation and cannot replace missing core evidence.

Every QB attribute has a five-tier hierarchy: current direct/tracking evidence; recent verified measurement or reputable charting; historical measurement or completed-season exact-trait evidence adjusted for age, injury, and role; recent play-level or credible film/charting evidence; and conservative bounded career-profile inference. EA ratings and locked OVR are forbidden substitutes. Low confidence blocks core traits, requires approved substitution for supporting traits, triggers uncertainty review for secondary traits, and cannot support any newly assigned 90+ scarcity rating.

The gate evaluates phase resolution, core coverage, bounded supporting substitutions, valid secondary paths, provider conflicts, positional comparisons, and scarcity enforceability. It reports explicit blocker and warning codes and does not approve, persist, or change workflow state.
